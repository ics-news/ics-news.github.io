The History of ICS and Round Square

What is Round Square?

Round Square is an international network of schools that share a commitment to the IDEALS — Internationalism, Democracy, Environmentalism, Adventure, Leadership, and Service. ICS Kumasi is part of this network, and the IDEALS you'll see across this site are drawn directly from it. Learn more at the official Round Square site.
