Round Square & More

The history, purpose, and values shaping life at ICS

What is Round Square?

Round Square is an international network of schools united by a shared commitment to experiential learning and character education. With more than 280 schools across 50 countries, it brings together students and educators who believe that learning should go beyond the classroom and into real, meaningful action.

At the centre of Round Square are the six IDEALS: Internationalism, Democracy, Environmentalism, Adventure, Leadership, and Service. These values guide the school community to be more holistic and internationally minded.

How is ICS affiliated with Round Square?

ICS is part of the Round Square community, joining in 2012, which means which exposed us to learn through action, reflection, and service. Through school programmes, projects, exchanges, and leadership opportunities, the Round Square philosophy helps us connect to the world.

In practical terms, this shows up in the way we engage with our communities, explore new ideas, care for the environment, and build confidence as leaders. The goal is not only to succeed academically, but also to become people who contribute positively to society.

Why it matters at ICS

The Round Square approach fits naturally with the values we as students have to carry beyond school, such as Integrity, Empathy and Compassion. It challenges us to think beyond ourselves, to listen to other perspectives, and to act with courage, responsibility, and purpose.
